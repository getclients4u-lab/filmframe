The Mission

No Studios. No Sponsors. Just Honest Takes.

FilmFrame was born from frustration. Too many movie reviews feel like press releases. Too many critics seem afraid to say a blockbuster is bad, or that an indie gem is great.

I started this channel to give movie fans what they actually need: sharp, honest analysis from someone who pays for their own tickets and has zero relationships with studios.

Every review is funded independently. Every opinion is my own. If a movie is bad, I'll tell you. If it's great, I'll fight for it.

01

Independent

No studio access, no press junkets, no sponsored reviews. I buy my own tickets.

02

Honest

If a $300M blockbuster is mediocre, I'll say it. If a $2M indie is a masterpiece, I'll champion it.

03
